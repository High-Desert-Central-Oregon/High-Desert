# Sign-in provider artwork

These locally bundled assets identify their respective authentication providers.
The marks belong to their respective owners; they are not Steppe artwork and are
not covered by Steppe's AGPL license.

- `google.png`: unmodified current G logo from [Google Identity](https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png), retrieved September 24, 2026. See [Google's sign-in branding guidelines](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines).
- `apple.svg`: Apple mark from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/apple.svg), retrieved September 24, 2026. The icon collection is [CC0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md); trademark rights remain with Apple. See [Apple's sign-in guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sign-in-with-apple).

The icons are decorative beside the localized button labels. Keep their original
proportions and colors. Provider availability remains controlled by the existing
server-side feature flags; adding artwork does not enable a provider.
